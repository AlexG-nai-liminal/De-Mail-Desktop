de-Mail Desktop

A local Gmail archive utility for Windows, published by Liminal. Copy original Gmail messages to a folder you choose, verify the saved files, and keep your archive on your own computer.

Production release in preparation. Public availability depends on Google OAuth approval.

How it works

  1. Click Connect Google account and approve Gmail read-only access on Google’s sign-in page.
  2. Choose a local archive folder and the messages to archive.
  3. Start the archive and review the verified .eml files and local manifest.

The app cannot send, change, trash, or delete your Gmail messages. Liminal operates no server that receives your email.

Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026.

What de-Mail accesses

When you connect Google, de-Mail requests gmail.readonly. This permits viewing Gmail messages, metadata, labels, and mailbox totals. It does not permit sending, changing, or deleting mail.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to select messages, save local RFC 822 .eml copies, create a local manifest, and verify those copies. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or model training.

de-Mail Desktop’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Local processing and security

Messages are processed on your Windows computer and written only to the folder you choose. Liminal does not operate a server that receives Gmail content, attachments, manifests, OAuth credentials, or archive destinations.

OAuth credentials are encrypted for your Windows account with Windows Data Protection API. They are sent only to Google’s official OAuth and Gmail API endpoints as necessary to perform the requested operation. Archive files themselves are ordinary local files; protect your computer and storage appropriately.

Diagnostics

No diagnostics are sent automatically. A local report redacts account addresses, credentials, authorization headers, paths, message filenames, and opaque identifiers. You see the complete report before choosing whether it leaves your computer. Update checks read public release metadata and do not automatically download or install software.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Liminal does not receive, sell, rent, or share your Google user data through de-Mail. Archive files remain until you delete them. Disconnect removes the encrypted local credential. You can also revoke access through your Google Account.

Contact and changes

Questions: alex@liminalmemory.com. Material changes will be dated and published before a later public release uses them.

Delete your data

Use Disconnect in de-Mail to remove its stored local Google credential. Revoke the application’s access in your Google Account’s third-party connections. Delete archive files and manifests from the folder you selected when you no longer need them. Liminal holds no server copy to delete.
