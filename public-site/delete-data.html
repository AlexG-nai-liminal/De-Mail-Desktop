Your data, your controls

Delete local data and remove Google access

Disconnect Google

Open Settings and choose Disconnect Google account. This removes the encrypted credential from your Windows account without deleting archives or Gmail messages.

To invalidate the grant at Google too, visit Google Account connections, choose de-Mail Desktop, and remove access.

Reset authorization

Choose Reset authorization to remove the local credential and reset the first-run privacy acknowledgement. The next connection begins at the privacy disclosure.

Delete archives

Archives are ordinary folders at the destination you selected. Delete them with Windows file management only after confirming that you intend to and that any required backup exists.

Complete local removal

Uninstall de-Mail Desktop, then delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\de-Mail Desktop. Uninstall deliberately leaves this folder so an upgrade cannot destroy history.

Liminal cannot delete Gmail data it never receives. Questions can be sent to alex@liminalmemory.com.
